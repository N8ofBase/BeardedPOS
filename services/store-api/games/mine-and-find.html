Mine it and Find it.

Flip cards, find your shape's treasure, and bump rivals back to the surface. Turn your phone sideways for the sideways map.

How to play

On your turn, tap a glowing space.
  • Miners move one space at a time, never diagonally.
  • Land on a card and it flips. Your shape? It goes in your chest. Not yours? It stays face up.
  • Land on another miner and they go back to their start. Then you flip or collect the card on that space.
  • Your Lamp lets you move diagonally. Your Cart lets you ride any distance in a straight line.
  • Find both and you get +3 and start the last round. Everyone else gets one more move.
  • Turn order is random each game. The chest at the top (or on the left, when the map is sideways) goes first.
  • Most treasure wins.
